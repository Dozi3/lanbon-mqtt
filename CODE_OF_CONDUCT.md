# Code of conduct

We want this project to be useful and welcoming to people with different
backgrounds and levels of Home Assistant experience.

Be respectful, patient and specific. Discuss evidence and technical tradeoffs,
accept constructive feedback, and help others understand how to reproduce a
problem. Respect people's time, privacy and control over their own devices.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal
attacks, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to disclose credentials,
household captures or proprietary material, or to operate physical equipment
without authorization.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, lock discussions,
or limit participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and
may range from a reminder to exclusion for serious or repeated violations.

For a concern that can be raised safely in public, flag the relevant discussion
to the maintainers without repeating private or harmful material. For sensitive
abuse or threats, use GitHub's reporting tools rather than publishing the
details. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
